Gate 02 / NightlyReview and propose—never block

Let the day settle.
Then look deeper.

Range

Review the day’s merged changes as one coherent unit.

Falsify

Challenge findings rather than asking a model to agree with itself.

Fix

Generate a narrow patch only when evidence and policy permit.

Propose

Open a PR. Repository-owned CI remains the judge. No auto-merge.

Measure severe findings and time savedNot comment volume
Gate 03 / ReleaseThe last gate cannot shrug

Review the release,
not just its PRs.

The unit of risk is the range from the previous release to the candidate—not the isolated changes that composed it.

SHIP

SIGN-OFF REQUIRED

Consequential uncertainty gets a named human decision.

STOP

Confirmed evidence crosses release policy.

05 / ArchitectureThe subject cannot redefine its judge

Separate the work from the authority.

Repositories own
  • Application source and architecture
  • Builds, tests, runtimes, and CI
  • Dependency and service setup
  • Normal workflow artifacts
Scruffy owns
  • Gate and escalation policy
  • Evidence requirements and interpretation
  • Analyzer, model, and prompt versions
  • Waivers, credentials, and audit history
Hostile-input boundary · agents hold no authority credentials
Policy is read-only to review agentsWrites use minimal credentials

09 / LessonsThe interesting parts were not the prompts

Trust is mostly plumbing.

01

Abstention is a feature

A system that can say “I do not know” is safer than one forced into confidence.

02

Durability changes the design

A webhook is only a prompt. The reconciler—not delivery—is the authority.

03

Models cannot grant themselves power

Deterministic evidence and policy must bound what model-backed analysis can do.

04

Passing tests is not proof

Generated fixes remain proposals because correlated mistakes survive green checks.

Evidence beats scoresOutcomes beat activity
